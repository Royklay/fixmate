How FixMate Works

Getting help with repairs is simple. Follow these easy steps to find the right professional.

1. Choose a Service

Browse our services and select the type of professional you need, such as an electrician, plumber or mechanic.

2. Find a Professional

View available provider profiles, compare their services and check their ratings.

3. Request a Service

Describe your problem, enter your location and select how urgently you need assistance.

4. Contact Your Professional

Use the available contact details to discuss the job and arrange the service with your chosen professional.
